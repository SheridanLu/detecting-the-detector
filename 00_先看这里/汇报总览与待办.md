## 二、文字页的英文稿

**指标定义(用在第 8、10、12 页右侧的表和 fig10/fig11):放在第 8 页的讲稿里讲一遍:**
- (第 5 页内容见 fig16,不需要另写文字。讲稿要点:三个模型 = 同一输入同一输出;逻辑回归是课程方法也是主模型;RoBERTa 和 Qwen 是老师口头同意的深度学习扩展;LoRA = 冻结大模型、只训练旁边一小层。)
- **False-flag rate**: share of real human essays the detector calls AI.
- **Precision**: of essays flagged AI, the share that are AI. **Recall**: of AI essays, the share caught. **F1**: combines both.
- **Bias AUC**: how often a TOEFL essay looks more AI-like than a native essay (0.5 = no bias, 1.0 = always).

**第 15 页 Challenges and what we learned(= fig15)**
1. Our first model looked strong (AUC 0.890) but a rough check showed it was about as good as guessing the corpus (0.888): we stopped using it as evidence and tested detectors on seven new data sources. *(We did not re-run it within one corpus; do not say we did.)*
2. RoBERTa learned a formatting shortcut (no paragraph breaks and curly quotes meant AI; AUC on same-prompt essays 0.04): we cleaned every text the same way and added a check; the AUC became 0.91.
3. Our first Qwen result (0% of TOEFL essays flagged) was measured on essays it had trained on: we kept TOEFL out of training and re-ran; it is 40% (two seeds: 56% and 23%).
4. Our native comparison was unfair: it looked like the training essays (2% flagged) while native essays of a new style were flagged 80–100% (logistic regression, RoBERTa): we added arms C and D.

**第 16 页 Recommendations and conclusion**
- A score is a prompt for review, never a verdict. Model results should support teachers' judgement, not replace it.
- Test a detector on your own students' writing before use.
- Training data must cover the writing it will judge.
- **Conclusion:** detectors flag the writing they have not seen; training on the right kind of essays cuts false flags in all three models, but not everywhere.
- **Scope:** our human essays were written before ChatGPT (30 Nov 2022); AI essays and detector scores come from the 2019–2023 generation. We did not test 2025–26 systems.

The team reviewed and checked all results and is responsible for the content. Qwen, RoBERTa and the open-source detectors are the subjects of our experiments, not authors.*

## 三点五、关于"调优"(被问到必须如实答)
- **我们没有做超参数调优。**三个模型的设置都是事先定的:逻辑回归沿用课程 notebook,RoBERTa 和 Qwen+LoRA 用常见值,没有在验证集上搜索。
- **为什么有意不调:**四种训练数据(A-D)要成为唯一的差别,每个臂各搜一遍参数会把差别搞混。
- **做过的决定:**训练数据变大后,RoBERTa 的训练轮数从 4 减到 2,Qwen 从 3 减到 1(没有用验证集验证)。
- **验证集(600 篇)用来做什么:**设第二个阈值(抓住 90% 的 AI);主结果统一用 0.5。
- **局限(第 10 页和第 16 页要提):**设置没有调优,调优后结果可能变;多个随机种子只反映运行间的波动,不代表换其他设置会怎样。
- 详细表格在备用页 fig17。

## 四、讲的时候的底线
- ❌ "我们的检测器比商用好":不是同条件比较(C 臂专门训练过考试式作文)。
- ❌ "检测器变公平了":C 只是放行了某一类文体,CS224N 仍有 97% / 70% / 23% 被判 AI。
- ❌ "一路进步":臂 A 比开源检测器差。
- ❌ "困惑度低就一定被判 AI":单篇作文不能下结论,看整体统计。
- ❌ "数据全部早于 2023 年":AI 作文和检测器分数就是 2019–2023 年的;能说的是"人写作文早于 ChatGPT"。
- ✅ Qwen 只有 2 个随机种子,要说明。
- ✅ 开源检测器的偏见分数(0.87 / 0.82)并不比最好的商用(0.84)低,它们的优势是误判少、抓 AI 准。
- ✅ Hewlett 数据集 88 篇里 85 篇含 @CAPS1 之类的匿名占位符,**不能用来比困惑度**。
